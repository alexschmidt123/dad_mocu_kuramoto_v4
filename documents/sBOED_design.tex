{\normalsize Sequential deep adaptive design for swing-equation probing}

\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

We study sequential Bayesian optimal experimental design (BOED) for identifying inertia and frequency-response parameters in a linearized swing-equation model.
The latent vector is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:theta_intro}
    \theta=(\mathbf M,\mathbf K),
    \qquad
    \mathbf M=(M_1,\ldots,M_{N_b}),
    \quad
    \mathbf K=(K_1,\ldots,K_{N_b}),
\end{equation}
with $N_b=14$ buses on the IEEE-14 benchmark, so $\dim\theta=2N_b=28$.
Each experiment applies a short, ordered sequence of probing actions; after each probe, a scalar frequency-stability feature is observed.
Designs are chosen adaptively by a history-dependent policy trained with sequential prior contrastive estimation (sPCE) in the deep adaptive design (DAD) framework~\cite{foster2021dad}.
The primary performance criterion is an sPCE lower bound on expected information gain (EIG) about the full latent $\theta$.
A secondary, simulation-only metric reports mean-squared error between the posterior mean $\hat\theta\in\mathbb R^{28}$ and the known test draw.

Throughout, ${\yclean}_t$ denotes the \emph{clean} forward-model output (noiseless max-ROCOF) and ${\yobs}_t$ the \emph{noisy} measurement used in the likelihood and in policy inputs.
Offline tables store both fields; the policy never observes ${\yclean}_t$.

\subsection{Physical model}
\label{sec:simulator}

Let $\Delta\omega(t)$ denote angular-frequency deviation from nominal.
The linearized swing balance is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:general_swing}
    M\frac{\dd \Delta\omega(t)}{\dd t}
    =
    \Delta P_m(t)-\Delta P_e(t)-D\Delta\omega(t),
\end{equation}
with mechanical and electrical power deviations $\Delta P_m$, $\Delta P_e$, and damping $D$.
The inertia coefficient obeys
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:M_def}
    M=\frac{2HS_n}{\omega_s},
\end{equation}
for inertia constant $H$, rating $S_n$, and synchronous speed $\omega_s$.
Following standard probing formulations for inertia estimation, the mechanical term is modelled as droop on frequency,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:pm_droop}
    \Delta P_m(t)=-K\Delta f(t),
    \qquad
    \Delta f(t)=\Delta\omega(t)/(2\pi),
\end{equation}
which yields the reduced equation
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:nrel_style_swing}
    M\frac{\dd \Delta\omega(t)}{\dd t}
    =
    -\Delta P_e(t)
    -\left(\frac{K}{2\pi}+D\right)\Delta\omega(t).
\end{equation}
For BOED, the probe design $\xi$ specifies an effective input $u_{\mathrm{probe}}(t;\xi)\equiv-\Delta P_e(t;\xi)$, so that
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:swing_model}
    M\frac{\dd \Delta\omega(t)}{\dd t}
    =
    -\left(\frac{K}{2\pi}+D\right)\Delta\omega(t)
    +u_{\mathrm{probe}}(t;\xi).
\end{equation}
Transient responses away from equilibrium encode information about $(M,K)$; between sequential probes the ODE state is carried forward rather than reset.

The prior is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:prior}
    \theta=(M_{1:N_b},K_{1:N_b})\sim p_0(\theta),
\end{equation}
with independent uniform components on bus-specific intervals.
Training and test draws use the same $p_0(\theta)$ but independent samples and noise.
At evaluation, uncertainty is represented by a \emph{particle posterior} on $\mathbb R^{28}$, not by a low-dimensional surrogate such as a single shared scalar $(M,K)$ pair.

\subsection{Design space, forward map, and policy}
\label{sec:sequential_design}

\paragraph{Designs and histories.}
The one-step design space is $\X$.
A probe at step $t$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:design_tuple}
    \xi_t=(a_t,b_t,d_t),
\end{equation}
where $a_t$ is amplitude, $b_t$ the bus index, and $d_t$ duration (fixed at $0.2$\,s in our experiments).
For horizon $T$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:design_obs_sequence}
    \xi_{1:T}=(\xi_1,\ldots,\xi_T),
    \qquad
    {\yobs}_{1:T}=({\yobs}_1,\ldots,{\yobs}_T),
\end{equation}
and the information state after $t$ steps is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:history_def}
    h_t=\{(\xi_i,{\yobs}_i)\}_{i=1}^{t},
    \qquad h_0=\emptyset.
\end{equation}
Repeated probes within one trajectory are disallowed: $\xi_t\in\Aset_t(h_{t-1})\subseteq\X$ excludes previously used actions.

\paragraph{Forward observation map.}
The forward function $F$ maps $(\theta,\xi_t,h_{t-1})$ to a clean scalar feature,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:F_def}
    {\yclean}_t = F(\theta,\xi_t,h_{t-1})
    = g\!\left(S(\theta,\xi_t,h_{t-1})\right),
\end{equation}
where $S$ is the swing-equation simulator producing $\{\Delta f(\tau),\ROCOF(\tau)\}_{\tau\in[0,T_{\mathrm{obs}}]}$ and
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:g_extractor}
    g(\cdot)=\max_{\tau\in[0,T_{\mathrm{obs}}]}|\ROCOF(\tau)|.
\end{equation}
Observations are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:yobs_noise}
    {\yobs}_t = {\yclean}_t + \epsilon_t,
    \qquad
    \epsilon_t\sim\N(0,\sigma_y^2).
\end{equation}
Because online simulation is prohibitive at training and evaluation scale, $F$ is precomputed on a trajectory bank: for system $i$ and design $\xi^{(j)}$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:F_table}
    \widehat F_{\mathrm{table}}(\theta^{(i)},\xi^{(j)}) = {\yclean}^{(i,j)}.
\end{equation}
Noisy values ${\yobs}^{(i,j)}$ are stored alongside ${\yclean}^{(i,j)}$ for each table row.

\paragraph{Adaptive design policy.}
A parameterized policy $\pi_\phi$ selects designs sequentially,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:dad_policy}
    \xi_t = \pi_\phi(h_{t-1}),
    \qquad t=1,\ldots,T.
\end{equation}
On the IEEE-14 setup we use three amplitudes, fourteen buses, and fixed duration, giving $|\mathcal{X}|=42$ one-step actions and $P(42,T)$ feasible no-repeat sequences (e.g.\ $68{,}880$ for $T=3$).

\subsection{Likelihood and sequential information objective}
\label{sec:likelihood}

Conditional on $\theta$ and the committed design sequence,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:history_likelihood_product}
    p(h_T\mid \theta,\xi_{1:T})
    =
    \prod_{t=1}^{T}
    \N\big({\yobs}_t;F(\theta,\xi_t,h_{t-1}),\sigma_y^2\big).
\end{equation}
Under an adaptive policy, $\xi_t=\pi_\phi(h_{t-1})$ and
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:rollout_likelihood}
    p(h_T\mid \theta,\pi_\phi)
    =
    \prod_{t=1}^{T}
    p({\yobs}_t\mid \theta,\xi_t,h_{t-1}).
\end{equation}
The marginal history density is $p(h_T\mid\pi_\phi)=\int p(h_T\mid\theta,\pi_\phi)p_0(\theta)\,\dd\theta$.

Before step $t$, belief is $p(\theta\mid h_{t-1})\propto p_0(\theta)\prod_{j<t}p({\yobs}_j\mid\theta,\xi_j,h_{j-1})$.
The one-step information score for candidate $\xi$ is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:conditional_eig}
    \I_t(\xi\mid h_{t-1}) = I(\theta;{\yobs}_t\mid h_{t-1},\xi).
\end{equation}
The $T$-step objective for policy learning is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:total_policy_info}
    \I_T(\pi_\phi) = I(\theta;h_T\mid\pi_\phi),
\end{equation}
equivalent to expected posterior entropy reduction,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:entropy_reduction_view}
    \I_T(\pi_\phi)
    =
    H[p_0(\theta)]
    -
    \E_{p(h_T\mid\pi_\phi)}\!\left[H[p(\theta\mid h_T)]\right].
\end{equation}

\subsection{Offline trajectory banks}
\label{sec:data_generation}

We construct two independent banks $\Dtrain$ and $\Dtest$ with identical physics, design spaces, and constraints but different seeds, $\theta$ samples, and noise.
For each training index $i$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:train_theta}
    \theta_{\mathrm{tr}}^{(i)}\sim p_0(\theta),
    \qquad
    {\yclean}_{1:T}^{(i)} = F(\theta_{\mathrm{tr}}^{(i)},\xi_{1:T}),
    \qquad
    {\yobs}_{1:T}^{(i)} = {\yclean}_{1:T}^{(i)} + \epsilon_{1:T}^{(i)}.
\end{equation}
Each system row in the bank lists all feasible no-repeat sequences $\xi_{1:T}$ with paired $({\yclean}_{1:T},{\yobs}_{1:T})$.
Test instances $\theta_{\mathrm{te}}^{(m)}$ are drawn similarly and reserved for evaluation only.
Horizon $T$ and sample sizes $N_{\mathrm{tr}}$, $N_{\mathrm{te}}$ are experiment metadata.

\subsection{Policy training}
\label{sec:spce_training}

Direct optimization of $\I_T(\pi_\phi)$ is intractable because $p(h_T\mid\pi_\phi)$ integrates over latent $\theta$ and stochastic design--observation paths.
We therefore maximize the sPCE surrogate~\cite{foster2021dad}.
For each training episode, draw a target $\theta_0\sim p_0$ and contrasts $\theta_{1:L}\sim p_0$, and form a history $h_T$ (designs from the bank; ${\yobs}_{1:T}$ from the same row).
The training objective is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:spce_objective}
    \LsPCE(\phi)
    =
    \E\!\left[
    \log
    \frac{p(h_T\mid\theta_0,\pi_\phi)}
         {\frac{1}{L+1}\sum_{\ell=0}^{L}p(h_T\mid\theta_\ell,\pi_\phi)}
    \right],
\end{equation}
The policy parameters $\phi$ are \emph{not} updated by backprop through $\LsPCE$ directly; instead, a scalar reward $\widehat I_{\mathrm{sPCE}}$ from the same contrastive likelihood supplies a learning signal for discrete action choices (Section~\ref{sec:policy_train_discrete}).

\paragraph{Information access at training.}
The network $\pi_\phi$ conditions only on past $(\xi,{\yobs})$ encoded as $(a_{1:t-1},{\yobs}_{1:t-1})$; it does not receive $\theta_0$ or ${\yclean}_t$.
In the sPCE reward, $\theta_0$ is the $28$-vector of the sampled training system and enters only through banked ${\yclean}_t(\theta_0,\cdot)$ in Gaussian factors.
Thus the reward scores how informative a realized $(\xi_{1:T},{\yobs}_{1:T})$ is about the row's latent, while $\pi_\phi$ learns which discrete probes to select.

\paragraph{Monte Carlo sPCE estimator.}
For fixed $(\xi_{1:T},{\yobs}_{1:T})$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:log_hist_likelihood}
    \log p(h_T\mid \theta,\xi_{1:T})
    =
    \sum_{t=1}^{T}
    \log
    \N\big({\yobs}_t;{\yclean}_t(\theta,\xi_t,h_{t-1}),\sigma_y^2\big),
\end{equation}
with centres from the bank (no online ODE).
The plug-in estimator is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:spce_mc}
    \widehat I_{\mathrm{sPCE}}(\theta;h_T)
    =
    \log p(h_T\mid \theta_0,\xi_{1:T})
    -
    \log\!\left(
    \frac{1}{L+1}\sum_{\ell=0}^{L}
    p(h_T\mid \theta_\ell,\xi_{1:T})
    \right),
\end{equation}
evaluated by log-sum-exp.
The per-step estimator is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:spce_step}
    \widehat I_{t,\mathrm{sPCE}}
    =
    \log \N\big({\yobs}_t;{\yclean}_t(\theta_0,\xi_t,h_{t-1}),\sigma_y^2\big)
    -
    \log\!\left(
    \frac{1}{L+1}\sum_{\ell=0}^{L}
    \N\big({\yobs}_t;{\yclean}_t(\theta_\ell,\xi_t,h_{t-1}),\sigma_y^2\big)
    \right).
\end{equation}
Reported stepwise and total sPCE-EIG average $\widehat I_{t,\mathrm{sPCE}}$ over $t$ and Eq.~\eqref{eq:spce_mc} over $h_T$, respectively.

\subsubsection{Discrete policy parameterization and training}
\label{sec:policy_train_discrete}

\paragraph{Discrete action catalogue.}
Each one-step design $\xi\in\X$ is indexed by an integer $a\in\{0,\ldots,A-1\}$ with $A=|\mathcal{X}|$ (e.g.\ $A=42$ on IEEE-14).
A fixed catalogue maps $a\mapsto\xi_a=(a^{\mathrm{amp}}_a,b_a,d)$.
At step $t$, the feasible set $\Aset_t(h_{t-1})$ excludes indices already used in $h_{t-1}$ (no-repeat constraint).

\paragraph{Stochastic policy.}
The policy is a categorical distribution over feasible indices,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:policy_categorical}
    \pi_\phi(a_t\mid h_{t-1})
    =
    \frac{\exp(\ell_{\phi,a_t}(h_{t-1}))}
         {\sum_{a'\in\Aset_t(h_{t-1})}\exp(\ell_{\phi,a'}(h_{t-1}))},
    \qquad
    a_t\in\Aset_t(h_{t-1}),
\end{equation}
where $\ell_{\phi,a}$ are logits from a history encoder and linear head.
Infeasible actions receive logit $-\infty$ before softmax.
Training rollouts sample $a_t\sim\pi_\phi(\cdot\mid h_{t-1})$; evaluation uses $a_t=\arg\max_a\pi_\phi(a\mid h_{t-1})$.

\paragraph{History encoding (inputs to $\ell_\phi$).}
For $t\ge1$, the network sees only prior \emph{committed} pairs $(a_i,{\yobs}_i)_{i=1}^{t-1}$.
Each step is embedded as $[\mathbf e_{a_i};\,{\yobs}_i]\in\mathbb R^{A+1}$ with $\mathbf e_{a_i}$ the $A$-dimensional one-hot of $a_i$; step embeddings are passed through a shared MLP, masked-averaged over time, and mapped to logits over all $A$ actions.
At $t=1$, $h_0=\emptyset$ and the encoder receives an empty history.
No component of $\theta$ or ${\yclean}$ enters $\ell_\phi$.

\paragraph{Table-consistent rollouts (no ODE at train time).}
After choosing $a_t$, the probe $\xi_{a_t}$ is fixed and the scalar ${\yobs}_t$ is read from the training trajectory bank by prefix lookup on the partial sequence $(a_1,\ldots,a_t)$.
The ODE is never run during optimization; the policy is trained entirely on offline $(\xi,{\yobs})$ pairs consistent with the banked forward model.

\paragraph{Reward for a completed trajectory.}
Fix a training system with latent $\theta_0$ and an realized length-$T$ sequence $(a_{1:T},{\yobs}_{1:T})$ (from policy sampling or from a table row).
The training reward is $R=\widehat I_{\mathrm{sPCE}}(\theta_0;h_T)$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:spce_mc}, with contrastive latents drawn from the particle support $\{\theta^{(n)}\}$ and all ${\yclean}$ centres taken from banked $F$ on that support.
The trajectory $(a_{1:T},{\yobs}_{1:T})$ is held fixed when evaluating contrastive likelihoods (Foster contrastive protocol).

\paragraph{Default objective: REINFORCE with sPCE advantage.}
Let $L_{\mathrm{pg}}(\phi)=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\log\pi_\phi(a_t\mid h_{t-1})$ along an \emph{on-policy} rollout where each $a_t$ is sampled from $\pi_\phi$ and ${\yobs}_t$ is table-looked up.
With Monte Carlo reward $R$ above and exponential moving-average baseline $b$, define advantage $A=R-b$.
One training step minimizes
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:reinforce_loss}
    \mathcal L_{\mathrm{REINFORCE}}(\phi)
    =
    -L_{\mathrm{pg}}(\phi)\,A.
\end{equation}
Gradients flow only through the sampled discrete actions (log-probability terms); $R$ is treated as a scalar weight (no differentiation through the sPCE contrastive sum).
Batches average $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{REINFORCE}}$ over several independent rollouts; Adam updates $\phi$ with gradient clipping.

\paragraph{Alternative: weighted behaviour cloning.}
When \texttt{objective=weighted\_bc}, each step samples a training trajectory row with probability proportional to its banked sPCE-EIG (curriculum over high-information designs).
For a row with index sequence $(a_1^{\mathrm{row}},\ldots,a_T^{\mathrm{row}})$ and noisy ${\yobs}_{1:T}^{\mathrm{row}}$, define teacher-forced history $h_{t-1}$ from the row prefix and minimize
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:bc_loss}
    \mathcal L_{\mathrm{BC}}(\phi)
    =
    -\sum_{t=1}^{T}\log\pi_\phi\!\left(a_t^{\mathrm{row}}\mid h_{t-1}\right).
\end{equation}
Here $h_{t-1}$ is built from $(a_i^{\mathrm{row}},{\yobs}_i^{\mathrm{row}})_{i<t}$; no sPCE multiplier is applied.
This phase imitation encourages $\pi_\phi$ to assign mass to empirically informative discrete probes before or instead of REINFORCE fine-tuning.

\paragraph{What is \emph{not} trained.}
The forward map $F$, the particle support, and the trajectory bank are fixed before policy training.
Contrastive counts $L$, noise $\sigma_y$, and catalogue $\{a\mapsto\xi_a\}$ are exogenous.
Test-time $\pi_\phi$ outputs the same discrete indices $a_t$, which are mapped to $\xi_{a_t}$ and evaluated on $\Dtest$ by table lookup.

\subsection{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:eval_plain}

At test time the ODE is not re-run.
Each method selects $\xi_{1:T}^{(m)}$ on instance $m$; ${\yobs}_t^{(m)}$ is retrieved from $\Dtest$ by prefix-consistent table lookup once $\xi_t^{(m)}$ is fixed.
True $\theta_{\mathrm{te}}^{(m)}$ is withheld from design selection and used only in scoring.

\paragraph{Particle posterior on $\mathbb R^{28}$.}
Let $\{\theta^{(n)}\}_{n=1}^{N}$ be a fixed support of $N$ training-bank latents, each $\theta^{(n)}=(\mathbf M^{(n)},\mathbf K^{(n)})\in\mathbb R^{28}$ with independent bus-wise entries drawn from $p_0$.
We assign uniform prior weights $p_n^{(0)}=1/N$.
After observing rollout $h_T^{(m)}$, the unnormalized posterior weights are
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:particle_posterior}
    \tilde p_n^{(T)}
    \propto
    \prod_{t=1}^{T}
    \N\big({\yobs}_t^{(m)};F(\theta^{(n)},\xi_t^{(m)},h_{t-1}^{(m)}),\sigma_y^2\big),
    \qquad
    p_n^{(T)}=\frac{\tilde p_n^{(T)}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\tilde p_j^{(T)}}.
\end{equation}
Thus $p^{(T)}$ is a discrete distribution on full $28$-dimensional particles; each particle carries its own per-bus $(M_b,K_b)$ and its own banked forward values $F(\theta^{(n)},\cdot)$.
Shannon entropy is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:entropy_def}
    H[p^{(T)}]=-\sum_{n=1}^{N} p_n^{(T)}\log p_n^{(T)},
\end{equation}
and reported uncertainty reduction is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:entropy_reduction_theta}
    \Delta H_{\theta}^{(m)} = H[p^{(0)}]-H[p^{(T)}].
\end{equation}
The myopic baseline updates the same particle weights sequentially using banked ${\yclean}$ centres on this support.

\paragraph{Primary metrics (information).}
For method-produced $h_T^{(m)}$, we set $\theta_0=\theta_{\mathrm{te}}^{(m)}$ in Eqs.~\eqref{eq:spce_mc}--\eqref{eq:spce_step} and average over $m=1,\ldots,N_{\mathrm{te}}$.
Contrastive terms draw additional $\theta_\ell\sim p_0$ (full $28$-vectors); sPCE likelihoods use banked $F(\theta_\ell,\cdot)$ on the training table.
Information scores $(\widehat I_{t,\mathrm{sPCE}},\widehat I_{\mathrm{sPCE}})$ and $\Delta H_{\theta}$ all refer to reduction of uncertainty about the \emph{joint} latent $\theta$, not about scalar summaries of $\theta$.

\paragraph{Stepwise EIG vs.\ posterior entropy reduction.}
At step $t$, the \emph{exact} expected information gain is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:step_eig_exact}
    I(\theta;{\yobs}_t\mid h_{t-1},\xi_t)
    =
    H[p(\theta\mid h_{t-1})]
    -
    \E_{p({\yobs}_t\mid h_{t-1},\xi_t)}\!\left[H[p(\theta\mid h_t)]\right].
\end{equation}
After observing a \emph{realized} ${\yobs}_t$, the plug-in entropy drop on the particle posterior is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:step_delta_h}
    \Delta H_t
    =
    H[p^{(t-1)}]-H[p^{(t)}],
\end{equation}
where $p^{(t)}$ is the belief in Eq.~\eqref{eq:particle_posterior} after $t$ observations.
One has $\E[\Delta H_t\mid h_{t-1},\xi_t]=I(\theta;{\yobs}_t\mid h_{t-1},\xi_t)$ under the correct model, so $\Delta H_t$ is a single-sample Monte Carlo counterpart of step-EIG, whereas $\sum_{t=1}^{T}\Delta H_t=\Delta H_{\theta}$ need not equal total EIG when nonlinearities accumulate.
We report:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
    \item $\widehat I_{t,\mathrm{sPCE}}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:spce_step}): Foster contrastive \emph{lower bound} on step information, evaluated at $\theta_0=\theta_{\mathrm{te}}$;
    \item $\widehat I_{\mathrm{sPCE}}$ (Eq.~\eqref{eq:spce_mc}): bound on full-history information (``Tot.sPCE'' in logs);
    \item $\Delta H_t$ and $\Delta H_{\theta}$: discrete entropy reduction on the $N$ training particles (``step-$\Delta H$'' / ``$\Delta H$'' in logs).
\end{itemize}
These three are aligned in spirit but not identical: do not equate ``sPCE-EIG'' with ``$\Delta H$'' without stating that the former is a contrastive EIG bound and the latter is plug-in Shannon entropy on a finite particle support.
Eval JSON stores per-step lists \texttt{step\_spce\_eig} and \texttt{step\_delta\_h}; summaries include \texttt{mean\_spce\_eig\_by\_step} and \texttt{mean\_delta\_h\_by\_step}.

\paragraph{Secondary metric (recovery in $\mathbb R^{28}$).}
The posterior mean is the barycenter of particles,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:posterior_mean_vector}
    \hat\theta^{(m)}
    =
    \sum_{n=1}^{N} p_n^{(T)}\,\theta^{(n)}
    \in\mathbb R^{28},
\end{equation}
with bus blocks $\hat{\mathbf M}^{(m)}$ and $\hat{\mathbf K}^{(m)}$.
Against the known test vector $\theta_{\mathrm{te}}^{(m)}$, we report
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mse_instance}
    \mathrm{MSE}_{\theta}^{(m)}
    =
    \|\hat\theta^{(m)}-\theta_{\mathrm{te}}^{(m)}\|_2^2
    =
    \sum_{b=1}^{N_b}\big(\hat M_b^{(m)}-M_b^{(m)}\big)^2
    +\sum_{b=1}^{N_b}\big(\hat K_b^{(m)}-K_b^{(m)}\big)^2.
\end{equation}
Component-wise summaries ($\mathrm{MSE}_M$, $\mathrm{MSE}_K$) average squared errors over buses within each block.
Higher sPCE-EIG does not imply lower $\mathrm{MSE}_{\theta}$.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We compare four strategies on identical $\Dtest$ instances:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,leftmargin=*]
    \item \emph{Random:} uniform feasible $\xi_t\in\Aset_t(h_{t-1})$.
    \item \emph{Fixed open-loop:} single $\xi_{1:T}$ applied to all test systems (default: first training-bank sequence).
    \item \emph{Myopic $\Delta H$:} greedy maximization of Eq.~\eqref{eq:myopic_delta_h} on the particle support $\{\theta^{(n)}\}$, with banked centres $F(\theta^{(n)},\xi)$ and plug-in mean $\hat{\yobs}=\sum_n p_n^{(t-1)}F(\theta^{(n)},\xi)$.
    \item \emph{DAD--sPCE:} $\xi_t=\pi_\phi(h_{t-1})$ with $\pi_\phi$ trained as above.
\end{itemize}
All methods share the same scoring routine for sPCE-EIG, $\Delta H$, and MSE.
The myopic score is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:myopic_delta_h}
    \Delta H_t(\xi)
    =
    H[p^{(t-1)}]
    -
    H\!\left[p^{(t-1)} \cdot \N(\hat{\yobs};F(\theta^{(n)},\xi),\sigma_y^2)\right].
\end{equation}
Here $p^{(t-1)}$ denotes the particle weights $(p_1^{(t-1)},\ldots,p_N^{(t-1)})$ on $\{\theta^{(n)}\}_{n=1}^N$.

\subsection{Summary}
\label{sec:logic_summary}

The proposed pipeline is: (i)~simulate and tabulate $({\yclean},{\yobs})$ for all feasible probe sequences under $p_0(\theta)$;
(ii)~train $\pi_\phi$ by sPCE with histories that expose only $(\xi,{\yobs})$;
(iii)~evaluate frozen policies and baselines by table lookup on $\Dtest$, updating a particle posterior on $\mathbb R^{28}$ with sPCE-EIG and $\Delta H$ as primary information metrics and $\|\hat\theta-\theta_{\mathrm{te}}\|_2^2$ as a secondary recovery diagnostic.
The forward map $F=g\circ S$ encodes swing dynamics and max-ROCOF extraction; sequential coupling enters through carried ODE state in $h_{t-1}$.
